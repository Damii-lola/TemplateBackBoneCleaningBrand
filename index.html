Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I book a cleaning?

Simply click the “Book Now” or “Get Instant Quote” button and fill out the short form. We’ll confirm your appointment shortly.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes. All our team members are fully insured, bonded, and undergo thorough background checks for your peace of mind.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. We bring all professional-grade equipment and supplies. If you have preferred products, just let us know.

What is your cancellation policy?

We understand plans change. Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice for cancellations or rescheduling to avoid any fees.

Do you offer recurring cleaning plans?

Absolutely. Weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly plans are available at discounted rates. Mention it when booking.

How long does a typical cleaning take?

It depends on the size of the property and the type of service. Most standard home cleans take 2–4 hours. We’ll give you a time estimate when you book.
